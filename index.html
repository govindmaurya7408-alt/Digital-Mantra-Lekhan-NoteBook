A GENTLE DIGITAL PRACTICE

About this notebook

Digital Mantra Lekhan is a simple, quiet space to write a mantra one line at a time. Every writing page includes one reference mantra and exactly 30 numbered lines.

Your pages stay on this device

Pages and progress are stored in this browser's local storage; they are not uploaded to a server. Browser-only data may be lost if you clear site data or use a different browser or device.

A gentle reminder

Copy, paste, cut, drag-and-drop, and the context menu are restricted inside the writing fields as a deterrent. These browser-level restrictions cannot guarantee that content will not be copied from another tab or through developer tools, browser extensions, or external input methods. They do not block typing, keyboard navigation, or Indian-language input methods.
